How sync works

  • Guest progress stays in this browser’s local storage.
  • When you sign in, local and cloud progress are merged (completions are combined).
  • Later changes sync while you learn — and you can force a sync anytime.
  • Signing out keeps a copy on this device; it will not keep updating the cloud until you sign in again.
