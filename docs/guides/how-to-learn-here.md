# How to Learn Here

Programming Foundations is built so you can learn **on this site** — courses, quizzes, progress, and guides — without being sent to other code-course providers.

## Your learning path

1. **[Start Here](../start-here.html)** — first steps in the browser.
2. **[Courses](../courses.html)** — pick Python, C#, AI, or an advanced language track.
3. **Lessons** — read in the course viewer; mark complete when ready.
4. **Quizzes** — check understanding without leaving the site.
5. **[Guides](../guides.html)** — deeper documentation that used to be “stretch” links off-site.

## Accounts and progress

- Guest progress stays in your browser.
- Optional [Account](../account.html) sync can follow you across devices (subject to free hosting limits).

## What we do not do

We do not redirect you to Udemy, Codecademy, freeCodeCamp, or similar platforms to finish our curriculum. Stretch reading points to **our Guides** library.
