[Slide 1] What this AI course is
This A I course teaches you to use language models like an engineer, not like magic. You do not need a coding install to start Module 1. Focus on clear goals, constraints, and tests.

[Slide 2] What LLMs are good at
Language models are good at drafting, summarising, and transforming text. They follow structured formats when you specify them, and they speed up iteration when you check the output.

[Slide 3] What they get wrong
They guess patterns; they do not know truth. They can invent facts, called hallucinations. Vague prompts produce vague, brittle results.

[Slide 4] The engineering loop
Use the engineering loop: spec, prompt, evaluate, iterate. Write the goal, inputs, outputs, and failure modes. Keep a small evaluation set and rerun it after every change.

[Slide 5] Your Module 1 next step
Open A I Foundations in the course viewer, copy the beginner starter pack templates, and write a one-page summariser spec with ten evaluation cases.
