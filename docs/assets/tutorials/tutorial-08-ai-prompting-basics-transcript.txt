[Slide 1] From vague to specific
Move from vague to specific. A good prompt states role, goal, and format. Add constraints like length, tone, and what not to do, and give one short example when helpful.

[Slide 2] Structure beats clever wording
Structure beats clever wording. Use headings for goal, inputs, and output format. Ask for checkable outputs, and prefer clear instructions over buzzwords.

[Slide 3] Test before you trust
Test before you trust. Try good, bad, and ambiguous inputs. Note failures like missing steps or invented facts, tighten the prompt, then retest.
