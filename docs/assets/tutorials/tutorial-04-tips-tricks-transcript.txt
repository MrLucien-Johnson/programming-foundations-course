Read each lesson, then take the module quiz. Continue banners bring you back where you left off, and certificates unlock when every module is complete.

[Slide 3] Guest mode vs free account
Guest progress stays in this browser. A free account can sync when the cloud is up. Keep guest progress as a backup on free hosting.
