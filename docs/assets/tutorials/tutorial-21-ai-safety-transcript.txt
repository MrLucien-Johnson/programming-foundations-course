[Slide 1] Today’s lesson goal
Today’s lesson goal: spot risky prompts and outputs. Add clear refusal and escalation rules. Keep user data out of places it should not go.

[Slide 2] Practical safety habits
Practical safety habits: do not paste secrets into prompts. Refuse harmful or disallowed requests. Document what the system must never do.

[Slide 3] Worked example: support bot
Worked example: a support bot may summarise tickets from provided text. It must not invent refunds or legal advice. It must escalate threats to a human.

[Slide 4] Practice checkpoint
Practice checkpoint: write a short safety checklist for your use case. Add two refusal test prompts. Then continue in the written Module 5 lesson.
