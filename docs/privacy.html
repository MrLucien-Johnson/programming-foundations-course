Privacy & your data

We aim to collect the minimum needed to run courses and optional progress sync. You can learn as a guest with no account at all — progress then stays only in your browser.

What we store

  • Guest mode: progress and quiz results live in your browser’s local storage only.
  • With an account: your email, a hashed password, display name, and your learning progress and quiz attempts, so we can sync across devices.
  • Organisations: if an admin adds you to an org, they can see your email, role, module completions, and quiz attempts for reporting.
  • Certificates: if you issue a verifiable certificate, we store the name, course, issue date, and a public verification code.

What we do not do

  • No advertising trackers or selling of personal data.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes (bcrypt) — never in plain text.
  • Admins in your organisation see progress metrics, not your password or unrelated accounts.

Data processing (DPA summary)

For organisations, Programming Foundations acts as a data processor for learner progress data on the organisation’s behalf. Data is stored in a SQLite database on our hosting provider. Access is limited to organisation admins (for their own members) and platform operators for support and maintenance. To request a formal Data Processing Agreement or a data deletion for your whole organisation, contact us via the Support page.
