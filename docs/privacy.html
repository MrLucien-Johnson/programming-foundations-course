Privacy & your data

Written for UK learners and organisations, in plain language for every audience. We collect the minimum needed to run courses. You can learn as a guest with no account.

Guest progress on your device

As a guest, you can allow this site to save learning progress in this browser on your device only. That lets you continue after a reboot without creating an account. Nothing is stored until you choose Yes below (or in the site banner). Nothing in the list is uploaded unless you later sign in and sync.

What can be saved on your device

  • Lessons you mark complete
  • Quiz scores and whether you passed
  • Start Here checklist ticks
  • In-lesson checklist ticks (outcomes / exercises)
  • Last lesson and last quiz (for “Continue where you left off”)
  • Optional learning persona (Schools / Workplace / Self-serve)

What guest mode does not save

  • Your name or email (unless you create an account)
  • Payment or donation details
  • Advertising profiles or third-party trackers
  • A copy on our servers (guest data stays in the browser)

Under UK PECR / UK GDPR practice we treat on-device progress storage as functional storage for a service you asked for (continuing your course), and we still ask for a clear choice. You can change your mind below, download a backup file you control, or create a free account for optional cloud sync. Children and schools: prefer a supervised account when an organisation needs records.

Backup you control

Download a progress file to keep after clearing the browser, or import it later. The file stays with you — we do not upload it unless you sign in and sync.

What we store

  • Guest mode: progress stays in your browser after you allow it. Optional JSON backup download/import is under your control.
  • With an account: email, hashed password, display name, and synced progress/quiz attempts for cross-device learning.
  • Organisations: if an admin adds you, they can see email, role, module completions, and quiz attempts for reporting.
  • Certificates: name, course, issue date, and a public verification code when you issue one.

What we do not do

  • No advertising trackers or selling of personal data.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes (bcrypt) — never in plain text.
  • Admins in your organisation see progress metrics, not your password or unrelated accounts.

Data processing (DPA summary)

For organisations, Programming Foundations acts as a data processor for learner progress data on the organisation’s behalf. Data is stored in a SQLite database on our hosting provider. Access is limited to organisation admins (for their own members) and platform operators for support and maintenance. To request a formal Data Processing Agreement or a data deletion for your whole organisation, contact us via the Support page.
